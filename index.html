Second modified live vaccine dose in adult shelter animals

Interactive companions to a modeling paper on whether adult cats and dogs in shelters need a second dose of modified live vaccine against panleukopenia, distemper and parvovirus. Each model estimates how many second doses a shelter would give to prevent one case, from its own length-of-stay records and case counts or from illustrative stays.

  • Second panleukopenia dose in adult catsDoses per case averted for your shelter, from your own stays and window cases, and a picture of which adult cats a second dose can still reach.
  • Second distemper and parvovirus dose in adult dogsThe same calculator and picture for adult dogs, for distemper and parvovirus.
  • Timing of the second doseWhat moving the second dose earlier or later than day 14 gains and costs, for cats and dogs.

Example record files show the layout the models read: cats, dogs. They are synthetic. Records you upload are read in your browser and are not sent anywhere.
